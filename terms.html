2. Purchases

Nine Bar Pro is available as a monthly subscription, an annual subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

Monthly and Annual Subscriptions

Nine Bar Pro is available through monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions. The price and billing period applicable to a purchase are shown before confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled in accordance with the settings and policies of the platform through which the subscription was purchased.

  • Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
  • You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Nine Bar Pro Lifetime

The one-time Nine Bar Pro Lifetime purchase is a single payment and does not renew. "Lifetime" refers to the commercial lifetime of the Nine Bar product and service, not the lifetime of the purchaser. It provides access to eligible Nine Bar Pro features for as long as Nine Bar is made available and those features or services continue to be offered. Features may change, be replaced, or be discontinued over time. See Section 8, "Changes and Discontinuation."

What Nine Bar Pro Currently Includes

Nine Bar Pro currently includes:

  • Unlimited equipment profiles. The free version includes a limited number of equipment profiles per category; Pro removes those limits.
  • Unlimited bean history. The free version includes a limited number of saved beans; Pro removes that limit.
  • Cross-device sync. Available only with Pro.
  • Trend charts and insights. Available only with Pro.
  • Custom fields. Available only with Pro.
  • CSV export. Available only with Pro.
  • Additional Pro features, as identified within the app.
Note: This list describes the Nine Bar Pro offering as it currently exists and may change over time; see Section 8, "Changes and Discontinuation."
